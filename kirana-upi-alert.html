Maal pack karte waqt QR scan ho jaye — PayEcho amount zor se bolta hai. Hardware ki tension nahi.

Play Store se lagao Hindi setup guide

Kirana dukaan mein roz kai baar UPI aata hai. Customer line mein hota hai, aap ration weigh kar rahe hote ho — aur phone silent mode pe rehta hai. Kirana UPI alert ka matlab yeh hai ki payment aate hi awaaz aaye, bina screen check kiye.

PayEcho isi problem ke liye bana hai: Android phone ko UPI soundbox banana, free plan se shuru.

Kirana counter pe kya problem hoti hai

  • Payment aa gaya, lekin screen pe nazar nahi gayi
  • Galat amount assume kar liya
  • Staff ne check nahi kiya, pehle customer chala gaya
  • Hardware soundbox mehnga / SIM / rent

PayEcho kirana ke liye kaise help karta hai

  • GPay, PhonePe, Paytm wagaira ke payment pe voice
  • Hindi / regional language options (app ke hisaab se)
  • Extra machine nahi — phone hi speaker
  • Free UPI soundbox plan se test kar sakte ho

2 minute setup (kirana style)

  1. Google Play se PayEcho install
  2. Notification permission Allow
  3. Language Hindi select
  4. Ek chhota test UPI bhej ke suno
Tip: Phone ko charging pe counter ke paas rakho, aur battery saver / “kill background apps” band rakho taaki alert miss na ho. Detail: battery guide.
