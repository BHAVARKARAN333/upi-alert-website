Payment Deducted but Not Activated?

Banking servers may take 10-15 minutes to confirm UPI/card settlements. Please tap Restore Purchase in the PayEcho app Settings, or wait 15 minutes and refresh this dashboard.

Billing, Payments & Refunds FAQ

Refund request submit karne ke baad kya hota hai?

Hamari billing team aapke order ID aur transaction details ko verify karti hai. Google Play ke rules aur payment gateway guidelines ke mutabik authorized refund 3-5 business days mein aapke original bank account / card mein credit ho jata hai.

Duplicate payment charge ho gaya toh refund kaise milega?

Duplicate payment hone par upar diye gaye Report Duplicate Charge button par tap karein ya support form submit karein. Extra deducted amount verify hote hi bina kisi hassle ke refund initiate kar diya jayega.

Google Play purchase ka receipt kahan se download karein?

Aapke registered Google email par Google Play ka official tax invoice pehle se send hota hai. Iske alawa aap upar transaction table mein View Details & Receipt button tap karke official PayEcho invoice preview aur print kar sakte hain.

Alternative Billing (Razorpay) subscriptions kaise kaam karti hain?

Alternative Billing transactions certified Indian banking payment gateways (UPI, Netbanking, Cards) ke through process hoti hain. Isme aapko Google Play payment methods ke alawa local payment options ka flexibility milta hai.

Kya main subscription cancel karne ke baad bhi purane invoices dekh sakta hoon?

Haan, cancellation ke baad bhi aapka account aur order history humesha secure rahegi aur aap kabhi bhi login karke purane receipts access kar sakte hain.
