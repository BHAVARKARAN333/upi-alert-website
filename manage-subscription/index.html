Subscription FAQs

Subscription cancel karne ke baad kya mera access turant band ho jayega?

Nahi. Cancellation sirf automatic recurring renewal ko rokti hai. Aapka Pro ya Business access aapke current billing period ke last date (expiry date) tak poora active rahega. Iske baad aap naturally Free plan par chale jayenge.

Google Play subscription aur website/Razorpay subscription mein kya antar hai?

Agar aapne app ke andar Google Play Store ke zariye purchase kiya hai, toh recurring payment Google manage karta hai aur cancellation bhi Google Play Subscriptions app se hoti hai. Agar aapne direct website ya alternative billing se payment kiya hai, toh hamara secure server dashboard use manage karta hai.

Agar mera payment fail ho gaya ya card decline ho gaya toh kya hoga?

Payment fail hone par subscription 3 din ke grace period / "On Hold" status mein chali jati hai taaki aapka soundbox achanak band na ho. Aap dashboard mein 'Retry Payment' button tap karke payment method update kar sakte hain.

Kya main monthly plan se Lifetime Access plan mein upgrade kar sakta hoon?

Haan! Aap kisi bhi samay 'Upgrade or Change Plan' par tap karke Lifetime Access (₹499 one-time) chun sakte hain. Iske baad aapko kabhi monthly rental nahi dena padega.

Refund kaise request karein?

Agar koi accidental purchase hua hai ya duplicate charge laga hai, toh hamare Orders & Support Page par jaayein aur Refund Request form submit karein. Hamari billing team 3-5 business days mein check karke authorized refund process karegi.
